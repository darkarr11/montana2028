Our labor should not be taxed. Our natural resources should.

The Flux Tax ends the income tax, the property tax, and the pile of government fees. Montana pays its way with royalties on the resources it already owns and a tax on new things you choose to buy. When there is a surplus, the rate goes down and the extra comes back to you.

Here is how it works. Read the whole idea in the book, or the law itself in the Montana Flux Tax Act.

The Montana Flux Tax, in short

One Fund Replaces Them All

Every tax and every fee you pay the state today goes away. Income tax, property tax, vehicle registration, licenses, permits, and every other charge stacked on top. In their place is one general fund, created and paid for by the Flux Tax. One source of money, one public ledger, and nothing hiding in the fine print.

No More Income Tax

Montana eliminates the state income tax. Your paycheck is yours. Every dollar you earn stays in your pocket. No withholdings, no filings, no audits. The state funds itself without taxing your labor.

No More Property Tax

Montana phases out the property tax on a timeline set by the Legislature. When you own your home, you own it. No more paying rent to the government on something you already bought. Retirees on fixed incomes stop living in fear of rising assessments.

Montana’s Resources Fund Montana

Mining, oil and gas, timber, coal, wind, solar, water — Montana’s natural resources generate royalty revenue for the people who own them: Montanans. The Legislature sets the rates per resource. Companies pay for what they extract. The money funds the state.

Consumption Tax on New Non-Essentials

A consumption tax applies only to new, non-essential goods and services. Groceries, medicine, housing, utilities, childcare — exempt. Used and secondhand goods — never taxed. You choose what to buy. You choose when to pay. That’s the voluntary part.

The Montana Resource Dividend

When resource revenues exceed what the state needs, the surplus goes back to the people. Every eligible Montana resident receives an annual dividend payment. Alaska does it with oil. Montana does it with everything.

The Flux: Rates That Adjust

If the state collects more than it needs, the consumption tax rate drops automatically. If revenues fall short, the rate rises. The system self-corrects. No emergency sessions. No budget crises. No political games. The math does the work.

Every Dollar Visible

All revenue and all spending published in real time on a public ledger. Every royalty payment, every consumption tax dollar, every dividend distribution — visible to every Montanan. No hidden funds. No slush accounts. Total transparency.

Used Goods Are Never Taxed

Buy a used truck, a secondhand couch, a refurbished laptop — zero tax. This promotes recycling, sustainability, and affordability. The tax only applies when something is manufactured and sold new. After that first sale, it’s free and clear forever.

Montana Stops Renting Its Future from Washington

A fiscal independence strategy to reduce reliance on conditional federal grants that come with mandates attached. Montana’s money stays in Montana. Montana’s decisions stay in Montana. The Legislature controls the pace.

The Legislature Decides Everything

This bill creates the framework — the committees, the processes, the mechanisms, the timeline. It does not prescribe rates, percentages, dollar amounts, or specific exemptions. Those are for the Legislature and the people of Montana to determine. This is the beginning of a conversation, not the end of one.

The bottom line: Your labor belongs to you. Your home belongs to you. Montana’s resources belong to Montanans. This bill builds the system that makes all three of those statements true.
